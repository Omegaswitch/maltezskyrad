## Users

Primary (user-confirmed): the general public — people looking up the Order of Malta in the Czech Republic, the faithful attending the Order's church of Our Lady beneath the Chain (Panna Maria pod řetězem) in Prague–Malá Strana, journalists, prospective volunteers and donors. Secondary: members of the Grand Priory, Church and partner institutions, authorities.

## Product Purpose

The official website of the Czech Grand Priory (České velkopřevorství) of the Sovereign Military Hospitaller Order of St. John of Jerusalem of Rhodes and of Malta. It identifies the Grand Priory as part of the single international Order, shows its hospitaller, charitable and religious work, keeps the faithful informed (services, concerts, news), and preserves its history in the Czech lands since the 12th century. Success: a visitor understands within seconds who the Order is and what it does today, finds service times and news, and recognises the site as part of the Order's international family (Grand Magistry communications policy, Feb 2025).

## Positioning

The only official Czech presence of a sovereign, 900-year-old lay religious order of the Catholic Church whose mission is "Tuitio fidei et obsequium pauperum". The Czech Grand Priory has been present in Prague since the second half of the 12th century (c. 1158–1169; recent research favours c. 1169, per M. M. Buben, 2026) at the church beneath the Chain and the Grand Prior's palace. No other organisation can truthfully claim this.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages (user-confirmed): language switch with Czech, English, French, Italian, German. Czech is the source language. On the user's instruction (2026-10-15) the Grand Priory's own pages are translated into EN/FR/IT/DE (content/translations/<lang>.json) and labelled "translation – awaiting review" until a native speaker approves them; Order-wide topics (mission, spirituality, history of the Order, Grand Master, government) link to the Grand Magistry's official texts in each language instead of being translated; news stays in Czech. Precise names and titles must come from official written sources.
- Czech full name (user decision, 2026-10-14): "Suverénní vojenský špitální řád svatého Jana Jeruzalémského, z Rhodu a z Malty" (form used in the Grand Priory's corrected 2026 Honours booklet). Other forms found in existing materials are not used for new site text; legacy texts keep their wording until editorial review.
- Official names: full name in the first instance and in the top banner; thereafter "Suverénní maltézský řád" (institution/diplomacy) or "Maltézský řád" (activities). Never "Malta" alone; no acronyms (SMOM, etc.).
- Two emblems only (institutional armorial bearing; red shield for activities). No emblem may be created, redrawn or approximated; labelled placeholders until approved originals are supplied.
- Official texts (mission, spirituality, governance, history, Grand Master, FAQ, contacts) must use Grand Magistry texts or link to them.
- Apolitical, neutral; no political opinion. No invented facts, statistics, quotations or endorsements.
- Must link to www.orderofmalta.int in the footer; top banner, colour scheme and general layout follow the Grand Magistry website.

## Product Principles

1. One Order: every page shows the Grand Priory as part of the international Order and leads to orderofmalta.int.
2. Service first: hospitaller and charitable work is visible before history, without diminishing the religious identity.
3. Truth over polish: preserve authentic content; mark everything unverified; never invent.
4. Maintainable by a small editorial team: monthly updates must be easy.
5. Dignity through restraint: the institution's seriousness is carried by structure, typography and real photographs, not ornament.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
